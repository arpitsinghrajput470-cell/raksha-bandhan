Dear Sister ❤️

Some bonds are not meant to be explained, they are simply meant to be cherished. And the bond between a brother and sister is one of the most beautiful gifts of life. ✨

हमारी नोक-झोंक, छोटी-छोटी लड़ाइयाँ, साथ में हँसना और वो अनगिनत memories... शायद मैं हर बार कह नहीं पाता, लेकिन ये सब मेरी life का बहुत precious हिस्सा हैं। ❤️

You have always been more than just my sister. You are a beautiful part of my childhood, my memories and my journey. And no matter where life takes us, this bond will always remain special. 🫶

On this Raksha Bandhan, I don't just wish you happiness for today... I wish you a life filled with dreams, achievements, peace, endless smiles and everything your heart truly deserves. ✨

Keep smiling. Keep shining. And always remember — your brother will always be cheering for you. ❤️

With Endless Love & Respect,
Your Brother ❤️
